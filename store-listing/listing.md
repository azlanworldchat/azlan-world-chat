## Full description

AZLAN WORLD CHAT is a global audio-only voice social app. Join live group voice
rooms, take a mic seat, chat by text while people talk, and send stunning
animated gifts.

- LIVE VOICE ROOMS — Create or join rooms on any topic, with 8 mic seats, host
  controls, announcements and room themes.
- TEXT + VOICE TOGETHER — Room chat stays visible while people talk.
- 54 ANIMATED GIFTS — From a 1-coin Rose to a 39,999-coin Shining Palace, with
  animated gift effects and big-gift broadcasts.
- VIP 1–12 — Badges, frames, entrance effects, mic rings, name cards, monthly
  golds and exclusive privileges.
- WALLET, COINS & DIAMONDS — Recharge, convert coins to diamonds, send gifts
  and climb the rankings.
- PROFILES, LEVELS & RANKINGS — Level badges, honors profiles, followers,
  friends, CP, families and agencies.
- SAFE & PRIVATE — Your email and phone number are never shown to other users.
  Block and report tools and owner moderation keep rooms safe.

Audio only — no video, no camera. Free to download; optional in-app virtual
items (coins, gifts, VIP) are for fun inside the app.
