# Privacy Policy — AZLAN WORLD CHAT

**Version 1.1.0 — 22 November 2026**
**Owner: Azlan**

## 1. Information We Collect

AZLAN WORLD CHAT collects the information you give us: your name, email, profile photo, chat messages, voice rooms you join, gifts you send and receive, and payment receipts you upload. We also collect device information (device model, app version) to keep the app working.

We do **not** collect your contacts, your location, or any data from other apps on your phone.

## 2. How We Use It

To run voice rooms, deliver gifts and coins, show rankings, and keep the community safe. We never sell your personal information to anyone, and we never show your email or phone number to other users.

## 3. Where Data Is Stored

Your account data is stored on the AZLAN WORLD CHAT server. Voice messages and photos you upload are stored on the same server.

## 4. Who Sees It

Other users see your public profile only: name, ID, level, VIP, badges and avatar. Your email, password, phone number, date of birth and gender are never shown to other users. The owner can see account details in the Owner Portal only to run the app and keep it safe.

## 5. Coins, Gifts and Payments

Coins, diamonds, gifts and VIP are virtual items for fun inside the app. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real money, except through the official recharge and withdrawal rules set by the owner. Recharges are approved by the owner after you upload a receipt. We never store your card or bank details.

## 6. Your Rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account at any time from Settings, or by contacting the owner through Customer Service in the app. We will answer within 15 working days.

## 7. Children

AZLAN WORLD CHAT is not for children under 13. See our Child Safety Policy in the app (Legal → Child Safety Policy).

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will show the new version in the app before it takes effect.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner of AZLAN WORLD CHAT through Customer Service inside the app.
